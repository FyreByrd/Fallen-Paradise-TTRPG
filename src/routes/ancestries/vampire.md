### Abilities

Blessing of the Moon: During nighttime you have temporary HP equal to `Passive Fortitude + PB`

Curse of the Sun: You are vulnerable to `Radiant` damage. While exposed to sunlight, take `d4` persistent radiant damage every minute.

Living Death: You are vulnerable to `Vitality` damage. Healing effects are treated as if they deal `Vitality` damage. `Necrotic` damage below `Passive Fortitude + PB` will instead heal you.

Hunter's Allure: You may cast Charm `PB + Will Bonus` times per long rest at no cost. The casting stat for this feature is `Will` if you have no spell-casting feature.

Bleeding Heart: As part of the vampiric ritual, you have a crystal for a heart. The crystal is considered a level 2 receptacle for decay mana and does not count against your receptacle limit. Take d8 persistent necrotic damage per minute if the crystal is damaged (d20 if it is removed or shattered). You must consume blood (or a suitable substitute) every `Fortitude Bonus + PB` days or the crystal will become broken. The crystal is automatically repaired upon consuming blood.
