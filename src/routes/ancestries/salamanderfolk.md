## Stats
- `+1 Fortitude`
- `+1 Free`

*Ancestry Hit Die*: `d8`

### Other Abilities
- `Natural Regeneration`: `LB` times per long rest you may:
	1. Regain health equal to a single roll of your `Fortitude` as a bonus action.
	2. Add a free surge to a `Fortitude` roll
	3. Automatically pass a death save.
	- Additionally, you may regrow a lost limb at the end of a long rest.

## Heritages:
- Fire: `Fire Resistance: LB`
- Tiger: `Poison Resistance: LB`
- Axolotl: `Water Breathing`
