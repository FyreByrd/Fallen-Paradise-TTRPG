## Stats
- `+1 Fortitude`
- `+1 Free`

*Ancestry Hit Die*: `d10`

### Other Abilities
- Do not need food. Can subsist on sunlight and water alone.
- `Shadow Vulnerability: 6 - LB`
- `Heat Vulnerability: 6 - LB`

## Heritages
- Ash: `Fire Resistance: LB`, No `Heat Vulnerability`
- Willow: `Acid Resistance: LB`, `Water Breathing`
- Oak: `Physical Damage Resistance: LB`
- Aspen: `Altitude Resistance: LB`
- Acer: No `Shadow Vulnerability`, can cast `Light` at no cost `LB` times per long rest.
- Acacia: No `Shadow Vulnerability` or `Heat Vulnerability`
	- `Thorns`: You grow long thorns. `LB` times per long rest, you may take 1 damage to add your `Fortitude Bonus` to a physical attack.
- Apple: You can grow up to `LB + Fortitude Bonus` apples on yourself. Eating an apple heals equal to a single roll of your `Fortitude`. Regrow `1d4` apples per long rest.
- Pine: `Frost Resistance: LB`
- Yew: `Necrotic Resistance: LB`
