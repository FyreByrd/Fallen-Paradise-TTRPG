## Stats
- `+1 Fortitude`
- `+1 Free`

*Ancestry Hit Die*: `d12`

### Other Abilities
- Do not need food. Can subsist on sunlight and water alone.
- `Shadow Vulnerability`
- `Heat Vulnerability`

## Heritages
- Ash: `Fire Resistance`, No `Heat Vulnerability`
- Willow: `Acid Resistance`, `Water Breathing`
- Oak: `Force Resistance`
- Aspen: `Altitude Resistance`
- Acer: No `Shadow Vulnerability`, can cast `Light` at no cost `PB` times per long rest.
- Acacia: No `Shadow Vulnerability` or `Heat Vulnerability`
	- `Thorns`: You grow long thorns. `PB` times per long rest, you may take 1 damage to add your `Fortitude` to a physical attack.
- Apple: You can grow up to `PB + Fortitude Bonus` apples on yourself. Eating an apple heals equal to a single roll of your `Fortitude`. Regrow `1d4` apples per long rest.
- Pine: `Frost Resistance`
- Yew: `Necrotic Resistance`
- Sumac: `Poison Resistance` (maybe also add a poison touch?)
