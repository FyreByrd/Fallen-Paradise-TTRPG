## Stats
- `+1 Dexterity`
- `+1 Acuity or Intellect`

*Class Hit Die*: `d8`

### Other Abilities
- Sneak Attack: If you are hidden from an enemy, you may target that enemy with a physical attack that the target cannot take a defensive reaction against. If the attack does not use `Dexterity` or `Acuity`, you may replace one of the stats used with either. You may also expend a surge die to add a number of surge dice equal to your `CB + Dexterity Bonus` to the roll.
- Acumen: During a rest, you may change the dice composition of a number of your stats up to `CB`. `CB` times per long rest, you may expend a surge die to increase 1 of your stats for a number of minutes equal to that stat's original bonus (minimum 1).
