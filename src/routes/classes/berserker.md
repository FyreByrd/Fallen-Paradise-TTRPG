## Stats
- `+1 Fortitude or Strength`

*Class Hit Die*: `d12`

### Other Abilities
- Surge Dice: `Passive Strength + Class Level * 2`
- Berserker's Rage: Whenever you use a surge die for Strength, Fortitude, or Will, begin `raging`.
	- While `raging`: 
		- Increase the size of your `Strength`, `Fortitude`, and `Will` dice by 1 size and decrease the size of your `Intellect` and `Acuity` dice by 1 size.
		- If any of your rolls that use `Strength`, `Fortitude`, or `Will` is less than your `CB`, you must expend a surge die.
		- You are immune to being `charmed` or `frightend`
		- If you take damage greater than your `CB + Fortitude Bonus`, regain 1 surge die.
	- `raging` ends at the end of your turn if:
		- You run out of surge dice
		- OR
		- You have not taken damaged or attacked an enemy since the previous round.
