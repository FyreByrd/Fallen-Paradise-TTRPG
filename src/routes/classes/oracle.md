## Stats
- `+1 Fortitude or Will`

*Class Hit Die*: `d10`

### Other Abilities
- Surge Dice: `Strength Bonus + CB + Will Bonus`
- Spell Casting: You are limited to casting spells of one elemental type chosen at Character Creation. If you cast a spell that requires more than one element, you can still cast the spell using solely the mana of your elemental type if that element is one of the required elements for the spell.
- Open Rift: As an action, you may increase your mana regeneration rate from 0 to 1. If you cast a spell that uses more mana than half your safe limit, your rift is automatically opened, but mana regen is still only increased from 0 to 1.
- Close Rift: As an action, you may reduce your mana regeneration rate. if you reduce by more than `Will Bonus` steps (a step is equal to 5), take damage of your elemental type equal to `the number of extra steps * your old regeneration rate * d12`
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, gain mana equal to your regeneration rate. Increase your regen rate by the level of any spell you cast.
- Safe Mana Limit: `CB + Passive Fortitude * 2^Surge Level`
	- At the end of your turn, if your mana exceeds this limit, take `d12 * the difference` damage.
- At levels 5 and 15 gain a level of resistance to your element's damage type.
- Oracular Surge: When you expend one of your `Will` surge dice while casting a spell, you may increase your surge level by 1 for a number of rounds equal to the roll of the surge die. When a surge level is dropped, you may expend a surge die to prolong it, or take a level of exhaustion.
	- While Surging, for each level of surging:
	- Your effective mana regen is doubled.
	- The fortitude portion of your safe mana limit is doubled.
	- Your safe upcast limit is increased by the surge level.
