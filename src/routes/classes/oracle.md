## Stats
- `+1 Fortitude or Will`

*Class Hit Die*: `d10`

### Other Abilities
- Spell Casting: You are limited to casting spells of one elemental type chosen at Character Creation. If you cast a spell that requires more than one element, you can still cast the spell using solely the mana of your elemental type if that element is one of the required elements for the spell.
- Open Rift: As an action, you may increase your mana regeneration rate from 0 to 1.
- Close Rift: As an action, you may reduce your mana regeneration rate. if you reduce by more than 1 step, take damage of your elemental type equal to `the number of extra steps * your old regeneration rate * d12`
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, gain mana equal to your regeneration rate. If you cast a spell at least one level higher than your regeneration rate, increase your rate by 1 step.
- Elemental Curse: At the end of your turn, if your mana exceeds your `CB + Passive Fortitude`, take `d12 * the difference` damage. Additionally, depending on your regeneration rate, the following penalties apply:
	- `&gt;2`: Your vision beyond 120 feet is obscured by your element.
	- `&gt;5`: Your vision is obscured beyond 60 feet. Deal passive damage in a 5 foot radius equal to your `Will`
	- `&gt;7`: Your vision is obscured beyond 30 feet. The radius and strength of your passive damage is doubled.
