## Stats
- `+1 Dexterity or Acuity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d10`

### Other Abilities
- Mana Limit: `Acuity Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Receptacle Limit: `&LeftFloor;CB / 2&RightFloor;`
- Surge Dice: `Strength Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Spells Known: `Passive Intellect + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Spell Casting: You may cast any spell you know that uses mana you have.
- Favored Enemy: You may choose a number of creature types up to your `CB + Passive Intellect`. You have advantage in tracking and knowledge checks for these creature types and can apply precision damage when attacking them. You may receive the same benefits for 1 minute for a different creature type by rolling a knowledge check with a bonus equal to the number of favored creature types in the same class. When adding a new favored enemy, you must have killed at least one member of the new type.
- Favored Terrain: Choose a number of terrains equal to your `Acuity Bonus`.  Same benefits as in D&D (for now). 
	- As a reference (from [DND5E Wikidot](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/ranger#toc6)): 
	> While traveling for an hour or more in your favored terrain, you gain the following benefits:
	>- Difficult terrain doesn’t slow your group’s travel.
	>- Your group can’t become lost except by magical means.
	>- Even when you are engaged in another activity while traveling (such as foraging, navigating, or tracking), you remain alert to danger.
	>- If you are traveling alone, you can move stealthily at a normal pace.
	>- When you forage, you find twice as much food as you normally would.
	>- While tracking other creatures, you also learn their exact number, their sizes, and how long ago they passed through the area.
	
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, regain 1 mana. As an action, you may gain additional mana equal to your `Acuity Bonus`.
