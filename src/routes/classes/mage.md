## Stats
- `+1 Acuity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d6`

### Other Abilities
- Mana Limit: `3 * (Acuity Bonus + Class Level)`
- Receptacle Limit: `&LeftFloor;CB / 2&RightFloor;`
- Surge Dice: `CB + Strength Bonus`
- Spell Casting: You may select up to 3 elements at Character Creation. You can hold mana of all the selected types on yourself. You can cast any spell that uses only the selected types of mana.
- Signature Spell: You may select a number of spells equal to your `CB + Acuity Bonus`. When you cast these spells you may subtract your `Acuity Bonus` from the mana cost (minimum cost 1). This feature may be used `CB` times per long rest.
- Meta-Magic: You can use any meta-magic option that you have mana for.
- Arcane Surge: As a reaction to being attacked, you may expend a surge die and add the result to a spell cast as the same reaction. Additionally, when you regenerate mana, you may expend a surge die to regain additional mana equal to the roll.
- Mana Regeneration: At the end of your turn, regain mana of one of your types equal to `&LeftFloor;CB / 2&RightFloor;`.  As an action, you may regain extra mana equal to your `Acuity Bonus`.
