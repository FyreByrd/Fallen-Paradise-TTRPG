## Stats
- `+1 Acuity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d6`

### Other Abilities
- Mana Limit: `&LeftCeiling;(Acuity Bonus + CB + Class Level) * 10 / #elements chosen&RightCeiling;` per element chosen
- Receptacle Limit: `&LeftFloor;CB / 2&RightFloor;`
- Surge Dice: `CB + Strength Bonus`
- Spell Casting: You may select any of the 10 elements at Character Creation. You can hold mana of all the selected types on yourself. You can cast any spell that uses only the selected types of mana.
- Signature Spell: You may select a number of spells equal to your `CB + Acuity Bonus`. When you cast these spells you may subtract your `Acuity Bonus` from the mana cost (minimum cost 1). This feature may be used `CB` times per long rest.
- Meta-Magic: You can use any meta-magic option that you have mana for.
- Arcane Surge: As a reaction to being attacked, you may expend a surge die and add the result to a spell cast as the same reaction. Additionally, when you regenerate mana, you may expend a surge die to regain additional mana equal to the roll.
- Mana Regeneration: At the end of your turn, regain `Acuity Bonus` mana of a number of your types equal to `Acuity Bonus + CB`.  If you have less mana types than the number that may be updated, you may regenerate extra mana equal to your `Acuity Bonus * the difference` of any types you have. As an action, you may regain extra mana of one type equal to your `Acuity Bonus`.
