## Stats
- `+1 Dexterity or Acuity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d8`

### Other Abilities
- Mana limit: `Acuity Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;` per element you have chosen.
- Surge Dice: `Dexterity Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling; + #elements chosen`
- Spell Casting: Choose 1 element at Character Creation. At every other level after, you may choose either another element or increase the size of your `Dexterity` or `Acuity`. You can cast any spell that uses your chosen elements.
- Meta Magic: You have access to any meta magic option that matches one of your elements and either deals extra damage or is `Slowing Spell`. These options can be applied to a melee attack. (When applied to a melee attack the cost is the same as a spell of  level equal to your `CB`).
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, regain 1 mana each in a number of elements you have equal to your `Acuity Bonus`.
- Martial Arts: For unarmed attacks, you may use `2 * Dexterity` instead of `Dexterity + Strength`.
- Empowered State: When you cast a spell of level equal to or higher than your current empowered state, you may expend the surge die of one of your elements for that spell to increase your empowered state for a number of rounds equal to the roll.
	- For each level of empowered state:
	- Increase Dexterity
	- Double movement speed
	- +1 action
	- The associated mana regenerates equal to `Acuity Bonus + #empowered state levels`
	- For every two levels of empowered state:
	- Increase safe upcast limit by 1.
