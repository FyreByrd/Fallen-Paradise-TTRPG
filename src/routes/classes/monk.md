## Stats
- `+1 Dexterity or Acuity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d8`

### Other Abilities
- Mana limit: `Acuity Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;` per element you have chosen.
- Surge Dice: `Dexterity Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Spell Casting: Choose 1 element at Character Creation. At every other level after, you may choose either another element or increase the size of your `Dexterity` or `Acuity` by 1, up to `2d20`. You can cast any spell that uses your chosen elements.
- Meta Magic: You have access to any meta magic option that matches one of your elements and either deals extra damage or is `Slowing Spell`. These options can be applied to a melee attack. (When applied to a melee attack the cost is the same as a spell of  level equal to your `CB - 1`).
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, regain 1 mana each in a number of elements you have equal to your `Acuity Bonus`.
- Martial Arts: For unarmed attacks, you may use `2 * Dexterity` instead of `Dexterity + Strength`.
