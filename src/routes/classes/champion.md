flavor: The champion is similar to a paladin in D&D or a champion in Pathfinder or a Cleric in either system. Rather than casting spells based off of affinity to one particular element or set of elements, champions hold to a specific ideal or abstract concept (each of which are associated with a specific element and two neighboring elements).
---
## Stats

- `+1 Will or Strength`

*Class Hit Die*: `d10`

## Other Abilities

- Mana limit: `Will Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;` per element associated with your primary principles.
- Receptacle Limit: `&LeftFloor;CB / 2&RightFloor;` (Remember, this *can* be a weapon...)
- Surge Dice: `Strength Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;` (I kinda want to factor `Will` into this as well, but I am not sure how. If so, I would probably need to rebalance the other half-casters a bit).
- Spells Prepared: `CB + Passive Intellect`
- Spell Preparation: During a long rest, you may select a number of spells to which you have access to, up to your preparation limit, as prepared spells. For each of your primary principles, you may prepare a single spell of level higher than your usual spell level limitations that may be cast once, with a reduced penalty: you may subtract your `Will Bonus` from the level of the spell when calculating the penalty.
- Spell Casting: You can cast any spell you have prepared any number of times, provided you have mana that can be spent. You have access to any spell that matches the elements of your primary principles, any natural school spell that matches the elements of your secondary principles, and School of Radiance spells if any of your principles match the the requisite elements.
- Principles: Choose one principle at character creation and gain its secondary principles. At levels 3, 8, 13, and 18, you choose one of your secondary principles to become an additional primary principle and gain its secondary principles. You may never choose a principle to be primary if it is an opposing principle of one of your primary principles.
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, regain 1 mana for each of your primary principles. As an action, you may regain additional mana in one of your primary principles equal to your `Will Bonus`.
