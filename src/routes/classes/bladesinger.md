## Stats
- `+1 Intellect or Strength or Dexterity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d10`

### Other Abilities
- Mana Limit: `Intellect Bonus + CB + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Receptacle Limit: `&LeftCeiling;(CB + Intellect Bonus) / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Surge Dice: `CB + Strength Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Spells Known: `Passive Intellect + CB + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`

At first level, choose 1 fighting style and 2 weapon spells.

At every 2 levels after, choose 2:
- 1 fighting style
- 1 weapon spell
- Increase Strength or Dexterity
- Increase Intellect

Weapon Spells may be cast as part of a weapon attack and have a cost reduced by `Intellect Bonus + Weapon Proficiency`

Mana Regeneration: Once per turn, when you attack, regain mana equal to your `Intellect Bonus` of a type you haven't used this turn.
