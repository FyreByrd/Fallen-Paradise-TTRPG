## Stats
- `+1 Strength or Dexterity`

*Class Hit Die*: `d10`

### Other Abilities
- Surge Dice: `CB + Strength Bonus`
- At level 1, choose 1 Fighting Style. At every 2 levels after, choose 2:
	- Increase Dexterity
	- Increase Strength
	- Increase Weapon Proficiency
	- New Fighting Style
