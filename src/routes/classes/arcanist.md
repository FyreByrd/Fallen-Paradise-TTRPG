Arcanists have no natural mana regeneration, but are able to utilize a significantly higher number of receptacles compared to other casters. This does mean that wizard could cast a larger number of much higher level spells, but also risks their magic being confiscated.

## Stats
- `+1 Intellect`

*Class Hit Die*: `d6`

### Other Abilities
- Mana Limit: `CB + Intellect Bonus`
- Receptacle Limit: `CB + Passive Intellect`
- Surge Dice: `CB + Strength Bonus`
- Spells Known: `Class Level + CB + Passive Intellect`
- Spell Casting: You can cast any spell you have mana for, provided you know it, and its school is not class-specific.
- Scholarly Specialty: You may select a number of schools, in which you know at least two spells, equal to your `CB + Intellect Bonus`. `CB` times per long rest you may subtract your `Intellect Bonus` from the mana cost of a spell (minimum cost of 1). If the number of schools you have selected is less than your `CB + Intellect Bonus`, you may additionally subtract the difference from the mana cost of a spell cast using this feature (minimum cost of 1).
- Meta-Magic: Meta-Magic options are also learned and count against your spells known limit.
- Arcane Surge: As a reaction to being attacked, you may expend a surge die and add the result to a spell cast as the same reaction.
