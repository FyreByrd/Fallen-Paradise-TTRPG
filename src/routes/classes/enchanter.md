## Stats
- `+1 Intellect or Will`

*Class Hit Die*: `d8`

### Other Abilities
- Mana Limit: `Will Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Receptacle Limit: `&LeftFloor;CB / 2&RightFloor;`
- Surge Dice: `Strength Bonus + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Spells/Songs Known: `Passive Intellect + &LeftCeiling;Class Level / 2&RightCeiling;`
- Spell Casting: You can cast some spells, but are limited to the schools of Voice, Thunder, Dazzle, Illusion, Wind, and Concealment. The mana stored on yourself must be Air. You are limited to casting spells/songs that you know.
- Musical Talent: You may choose a number of songs you know equal to your `CB + Intellect Bonus` in which you now have Expertise. Above 10th level, you may instead select a song you already have Expertise in to receive Mastery in. Above 15th level, you may instead become Legendary in a song you already have Mastery in.
- Taunt: As an action, you may taunt an enemy to inflict `Will + Intellect + CB` damage.
- Mana Regeneration: At the start of your turn, regain 1 Air mana. As an action, you may regain Air mana equal to your `CB`.
