## Natural Schools

Druids and Rangers are limited to these schools. I may also limit Clerics to these schools, but I haven't yet figured out how Clerics are supposed to work. The below diagram illustrates the relationships between the natural schools.

![A really cool diagram](/natural-schools.png)

The schools, as well as sample capabilities are as follows.
- Flame (F): Produce natural appearing flame or control natural flames
- Waves (W): Produce or control exposed water
- Form (E): Manipulate the form of dirt or stone
- Wind (A): Produce or manipulate gusts of wind
- Power (L): Produce lightning and simple light effects
- Concealment (S): Produce or control supernatural fog made of shadow itself
- Vitality (N): Healing
- Transformation (N): Acquire beast-like features and abilities
- Growth (N): Manipulate plant growth
- Frost (I): Create cold and snow/ice effects
- Purification (F + N): Cauterize wounds and purify water
- Weather (W + A): Control the weather
- Tectonics (F + E): Rend the earth itself and bring forth lava
- Decay (D): Cause items or corpse to decay quickly
- ? Night (S): Force a creature to sleep. Moonbeam???

## Class-Specific Schools

These schools are limited to specific classes.
- Artifice (S): Limited to Artificer. Bind magical energies to physical objects.
- Radiance (L + F): Limited to some sub-classes of Champion, Cleric (if added), Monk, and Oracle. Create light so powerful you hold the power of the sun in the palm of your hand.
- Voice (A): Limited to Enchanter. Magical manipulation of the voice. May include songs?

## Arcane Schools

These schools describe magic that is an unusual application or combination of magical energy.
- Destruction (F): Effects that are more explosive/destructive than mere flames.
- Heat (F): Magically heat metal or see heat. Might be rolled into Metallurgy.
- Absorption (W): Magically dissolve materials through caustic effects.
- Force (E): Catapult, shock wave, etc.
- Kinesis (E): telekinesis etc.
- Thunder (A): really loud
- Dazzle (L): Light effects and dazzling flashes
- Invisibility (L): invisibility. May be reassigned to either `Shadow` or `Shadow + Light`.
- Illusion (S): Illusions. May be reassigned to either `Light` or `Shadow + Light`.
- Binding (S): Magical ropes
- Protection (I): Magical armor and shields
- Teleportation (T): teleportation. May be moved to Natural School.
- Chronurgy (T): time manipulation. Definitely includes speeding and slowing of time, as well as TotK style recall. The jury is still out on abilities such as Stasis/Time Stop and King Crimson.
- Death (D): Really powerful damage spells. IDK what else. *Maybe* necromancy?
- Metallurgy (L + E): Manipulate metal.
- Crystal (I + E): Manipulate crystalline material.
- Anti-Magic (T + I): Block the casting of spells.
- ? Creation (S + E): Create realistic objects out of wisps and matter.
- ? Noxion (D + A): Poisonous gasses
- ? Poison (D + W): Create poisons
