Meta-magic options can be utilized at the same time as the casting of a spell to modify some aspects of the casted spell. The cost of a meta-magic option is an amount of mana equal to the level of the spell it is modifying. For some meta-magic options, the type of mana to be expended is specified, whereas in some other cases the type of mana will match the type of the spell to be modified.
- Twinned Spell (match): Target a second creature in range with the same spell. Can only be applied to a spell that targets a single creature and has a range greater than self.
- Heated Spell (F): Add extra fire damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat. Cannot be used with a spell that uses `Ice`.
- Frosty Spell (I): Add extra frost damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat. Cannot be used with a spell that uses `Fire`.
- Caustic Spell (W): Add extra acid damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat.
- Forceful Spell (E): Add extra force damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat. Knock back targets by `5 * spell level` feet.
- Deafening Spell (A): Add extra thunder damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat. Targets are Deafened 1.
- Shocking Spell (L): Add extra lightning damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat.
- Blinding Spell (L): Add extra radiant damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat. Targets are Blinded 1.
- Blinding Spell (S): Add extra necrotic damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat. Targets are Blinded 1.
- Vitalizing Spell (N): Add extra vitality damage (or healing effect) equal to a single roll of your casting stat.
- Necrotizing Spell (D): Add extra necrotic damage equal to a single roll of your casting stat.
- Slowing Spell (T): Targets are Slowed 1.
- Hastening Spell (T): Targets are Hastened 1.
- Grappling Spell (S or N): Targets are grappled.
- Distant Spell (A or L): Spell range is doubled. Range of touch is extended to 30 feet.
- Careful Spell (I): Choose a number of creatures you can see up to your spell casting bonus. Chosen creatures have advantage + your spell casting modifier to avoid the effects of the spell.
- Extended Spell (T): Spell duration is doubled.
- Quickened Spell (T): Spell casting time is halved. Casting time of 1 action is reduced to a bonus action.
- Expanded Spell (match): Spell effect area is doubled.
- Empowered Spell (match): You may re-roll a number of dice used in the casting of the spell up to your casting modifier. You must take the new result.
- Jumping Spell (L): The spell jumps from the target to a new creature within 5 feet. Roll `1d10`. If the result is less than or equal to your casting modifier, the spell jumps to a new creature within 5 feet. Repeat until the `1d10` roll fails. Can only be a spell that targets 1 creature and has immediate duration.
- Subtle Spell (S): Spell is cast without verbal or somatic components. (I haven't actually figured out yet how spells work with verbal, somatic, and material components),
