A player character starts at level 1 and can level up all the way to level 40.
A player character must have at least 1 class, and can have as many classes as they feel capable of keeping track of, provided other class-specific restrictions are met.
For each class a character has, the character has a different level in each class, the sum of which is equal to their character level. An individual class can only be leveled up to 20. A character can only level up one of their classes at a time when leveling up their character. Additionally, every 4th level, starting at level 4 a character may increase the size of up to two of their stats by 1 step or one stat by 2 steps (i.e. at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, and 40) for a total of 10 possible level ups.

## Proficiency Bonuses

As 20 is a rather large number to be applying as a bonus to a roll, even at high levels, a character's proficiency bonus is scaled based on their level, similar to in D&D.
A character further has two different kinds of proficiency bonuses:
1. Player Bonus (`PB`) which scales according to the character level
2. Class Bonus (`CB`) which scales to the character's level in a class, for each class.

In either case, the formula for calculating the bonus is the same: `&LeftCeiling;level / 4&RightCeiling;`. A table is provided below for convenience.

| *Level* | *Bonus* | *Level* | *Bonus* |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 1-4     | +1      | 21-24   | +6      |
| 5-8     | +2      | 25-28   | +7      |
| 9-12    | +3      | 29-32   | +8      |
| 13-16   | +4      | 33-36   | +9      |
| 17-20   | +5      | 37-40   | +10     |
